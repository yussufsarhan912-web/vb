Before we work together

Clear answers.

What do I still have to do?

Bring your perspective and create the core content. You stay involved in the direction and the approvals that matter; we coordinate the work around it. Your exact responsibilities are agreed before we begin.

Can you work with my existing editor?

Yes. We can build around an existing team and take responsibility for the parts you need help with. We agree ownership and handoffs so work stays coordinated.

Do you only work with large creators?

No. We look for a clear direction, a commitment to creating and a genuine fit for our support. Tell us where you are now and what you want to build.

How does the one-week trial work?

For suitable creators, we agree a focused one-week scope and a case-study permission agreement before starting. After the trial, you decide whether to continue with a paid plan. There is no automatic paid renewal.

How do revisions and delivery work?

The number of revisions, deliverables and turnaround times are agreed in your scope. Your team shares current status, deadlines and the items that need your review.

Do you guarantee views or brand deals?

No. Content performance and sponsorship opportunities depend on the audience, topic, market and many other factors. We commit to the agreed work, thoughtful execution and learning from the results.

How do sponsorships work?

We help with relevant brand targeting, outreach, negotiation, coordination and payment tracking. Commercial terms and any commission are agreed separately before work begins.

Can I cancel?

Billing, notice and cancellation terms are set out in your individual agreement before you become a paying client.
